Gemini Live — voice-to-voice POC

mic → Gemini Live → speaker. One socket. No Oscar agent, no LangGraph, no tools, nothing persisted. The MODEL is chosen server-side by GEMINI_LIVE_MODEL (default gemini-3.1-flash-live-preview, which measured ~1.2 s to first audio versus 3–5 s on the 2.5 native-audio models) — the browser cannot pick it, because a client that could choose the model could bill a pricier one to our key.
Experiment. The backend needs ENABLE_GEMINI_LIVE=1 and GEMINI_API_KEY set, or this page cannot connect. Use headphones — the microphone stays open through the reply so that barge-in works, and on laptop speakers Gemini can hear itself and interrupt its own answer.

Tools: a user_id is required and the backend must run with GEMINI_LIVE_TOOLS=1. Blank means Gemini can talk but cannot touch the workspace — deliberately fail-closed, since every Oscar tool is scoped to a user. Whatever id you enter acts for real: tasks are created, notifications fire at that person's phone. The Log below prints every ask_oscar round trip.
